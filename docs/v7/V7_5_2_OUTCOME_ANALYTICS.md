# V7.5.2 Outcome Analytics

V7.5.2 adds read-only outcome summaries from explicit trip-completion records.
It reports planned reports, completed outcomes, fished and did-not-fish trips,
no-catch trips, trips with catches, catch success, catches per fished trip, and
optional satisfaction. Did-not-fish records are excluded from fishing-success
denominators, and missing completion records are never interpreted as failures.

The dashboard places this material inside the existing collapsed Personal
Evidence disclosure. It does not change Smart Intelligence or live ranking.
