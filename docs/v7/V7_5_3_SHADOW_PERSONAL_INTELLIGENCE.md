# V7.5.3 Shadow Personal Intelligence

V7.5.3 calculates a personal-evidence adjustment without applying it to Smart
Intelligence, water ranking, or any live recommendation. Comparable outcomes
are only fished trips with direct `exact` or `partial` adherence. Did-not-fish
records and changed plans are excluded from comparable evidence.

Fewer than three comparable trips yields no signal. Three through seven are
exploratory. Eight or more are eligible for a later guarded review. Every
proposed adjustment is capped at plus or minus five and displayed in the
existing collapsed Personal Evidence area.
